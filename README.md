# OTA Atlas

A dashboard of online travel agents (OTAs) across 10 Asian markets: China, Japan, Korea, Taiwan, Hong Kong, Indonesia, Vietnam, the Philippines, Thailand and Malaysia.

**Live page:** https://eadgpt.github.io/ota-atlas/

## What's inside
- Who the OTAs are, who owns them, and who is rising
- Market share, and how each share figure is measured
- Members, users and customer segments
- Product strengths and app features
- Partnerships with tourism boards, airports, airlines and hotels
- Explore: footprint map, partner finder and side-by-side compare
